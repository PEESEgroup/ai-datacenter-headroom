# Third-party data and attribution

The MIT license applies to author-written code, not to every data file in this
repository. Source data retain their providers' rights and conditions. Source
identities, vintages and processing are retained in the source manifests,
protocol files and `INPUTS_AND_SCOPE.md`.

- **FracTracker Alliance:** retained project records derive from the Open U.S.
  Data Centers Tracker snapshot used in the study. FracTracker permits sharing
  for non-commercial use with attribution. Credit FracTracker Alliance and retain
  those conditions; this package does not grant commercial reuse rights.
  Source and sharing statement: https://fractracker.org/data-centers/
- **IM3/Open Source Data Center Atlas:** existing-facility geography retains its
  source attribution and original dataset terms.
- **NERC:** planning inputs and retained source assessments remain NERC material.
  Source references and extraction locations accompany the numerical inputs.
  No independent license to redistribute other NERC products is granted here.
- **EIA:** generator inventories and reported hourly demand are attributed to
  the U.S. Energy Information Administration. The archived observations are not
  guaranteed free of source measurement errors.
- **NASA POWER and ERA5:** source-matched weather retains attribution to NASA
  POWER and Copernicus/ECMWF ERA5. Weather files are standardized analysis inputs,
  not a redistribution of the full provider archives.
- **ISO/RTO market data:** retained standardized historical price/load panels are
  attributed to the providers in their source manifests and retain provider use
  conditions. This release does not grant rights to redistribute other provider
  downloads or represent the data as a commercial market-data service.

Processed tables and factual measurements should be reused with the study and
source attribution. Do not assume that the code license changes upstream terms.
