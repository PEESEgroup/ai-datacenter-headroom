# Inputs, derivations and reproduction boundary

## Framework rules

FRAMEWORK_RULES.json records the existing analysis rules and their interpretation.
The national paths describe total non-mining facility electricity use, with
overhead already included. The project inventory supplies conditional location
weights, not a measured electricity inventory. Its reported MW can denote IT
capacity, facility electrical demand or announced buildout; unspecified scales
remain proxies rather than being silently converted or deleted. Confirmed
generation attributes retain the documented corrections.

The paired candidate increment assumes grid supply in the assigned region.
This is a demand scenario, not a forecast of each campus's net imports. On-site
generation is not automatically credited to the official resource path. The
same regional weights define the candidate's 2025 anchor and later growth;
neither is presented as an observed 2025 regional distribution.

The county display is separate: its 2025 within-region shape retains the archived
effective-area distribution, normalized to scenario regional totals. Later county
growth follows reviewed project weights. Both the downscaling update and the
capacity review affect county concentration. County values do not determine
regional candidate increments or official-demand balances.

The default geographic proxy uses a broader inventory than the non-mining energy
category. Existing mining-scope, campus and raw-attribute alternatives test
regional redistribution at fixed national energy and capture. They are not a
national non-mining denominator reconstruction. The raw-attribute row at fixed
capture is also distinct from the complete old-default comparison, which retains
its own capture. These separate questions are not combined into a claim of
observed grid geography.

## Planning components

`numerical/inputs/native_summer_components.csv` contains the NERC 2025 LTRA
summer source components for seven planning areas, 2026-2035. It is extracted
with the original native-ledger parser from the archived source report, and
contains source demand, response, resource tiers, transfers, reported reserve
margins and capacity-basis metadata. It contains no final scenario balances.
Page numbers, the source URL and extraction equations remain in the ledger code.

`ercot_reported_response.csv` contains the separately reported ERCOT large-load
and provisional-curtailment table, including its source page. Ordinary response
and the alternative crediting conditions are calculated during reproduction.

`source_planning_components.csv` retains the pre-existing source-path components
required for the original LBNL scenario builder's lineage calculation. Only
demand, response, resources, reference levels and identifiers are exported from
that older harmonized panel: no balances, contributions or thresholds are input.
The runner recomputes its balances and legacy timing rules, then applies the
unchanged seasonal ledger to the matched analysis.

`embedded_DC_paths.csv` contains the previously harmonized annual source-path
bounds and identification classes. Annual source interpolation, PJM's limited
floor and NYISO's mixed-load bound are preprocessing inputs, not new observed
outcomes. All paired balances and linked-response bounds are rebuilt, rather
than copied from the earlier counterfactual table.

## Other numerical inputs

- LBNL anchors and fixed scenario extensions are in `numerical/sources` and
  `numerical/config`. Historical/digitized/author-extended values stay labeled.
- Source-matched load-shape profiles and peak windows retain their archived
  files. Shape-to-peak mapping is rebuilt by the existing mapping script.
- Exported project records and frozen geographic crosswalks are in
  `numerical/spatial`. MW parsing, scope alternatives, co-location checks and
  eight allocation rules are recomputed. Frozen actual grid connections are not
  certified by a reproducible county crosswalk.
- The historical forecast and outcome panels are harmonized inputs; model
  fits and holdouts are repeated. Re-extraction of all historical PDFs is not
  claimed by the default run.
- Hourly weather inputs retain GHI, temperature, wind and UTC labels, not stored
  PV dispatch outputs. The physical conversion and storage dispatch are repeated.
  NASA/ERA5 original bulk archives are not needed for this standard-input run.
- EIA reported-demand archives support season-quality checks and peak-hour
  selection. Anomaly and missing-season exclusions remain the frozen rules.
- Separate inventory and price support modules include their required source
  snapshots and harmonized hourly panels. They are not substituted into the
  main regional supply path.

## Outputs versus fixtures

This public release includes standardized inputs and figure/table data, but no author manuscript or SI.
Full-precision scientific results are generated by the supported runner.
The supported runner recomputes native, paired, threshold and scenario tables.
Frozen `expected` and `regression` files are immutable comparison targets.
Some support physical tables specify the configurations to simulate; their
reported result columns are used only as comparisons, not as numerical inputs
to the dispatch. Retained figure data are also comparison targets for newly
exported tables, never upstream balance inputs.

Descriptive county allocations, nuclear inclusion/context and certain
source-audit tables remain explicit supporting data. The default run does not
rebuild the entire worldwide facility inventory or infer nuclear net additions
that the manuscript deliberately leaves unquantified. It also does not render
publication figures or compile the manuscript.

## California alignment

NERC 2025 LTRA physical p.147 lists CAISO, LADWP, TID and BANC within the new
WECC-California assessment area. The working proxy unions their HIFLD polygons
(validation 2022-06-03). The source wording is not treated as proof of exhaustive
membership. IID inclusion is therefore an explicit alternative, not a silent
change. This layer neither certifies the precise 2025 boundary nor establishes
each project's actual electrical connection.

The frozen `alignment/project_footprint_crosswalk.csv` preserves record keys,
old/new geographic membership and explicit verification flags. Core/broad/count
capture fractions and eight weights are recomputed together. The California
peak window is transferred from the archived CAISO shape as a scenario only;
it is not called an observed WECC-California noncoincident-peak estimate.
Historical generator-inventory and CAISO price-market support remain at their
original measured scope. They are not relabeled as broader WECC observations.

## Release permissions

Source-specific links, hashes and dates are retained. Processing does not erase
third-party terms. The project registry retains FracTracker's credited non-commercial sharing
terms; standardized historical market panels retain provider rights. They
are not made MIT-licensed by this package. See THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md. No access or rights restriction is inferred solely from file size or
the presence of facility coordinates.
